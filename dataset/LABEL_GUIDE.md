# Label Guide

## Goal

Label momentary study/work state, not personality and not clinical mental health.

## Priority rule

When more than one signal appears, use this priority unless context strongly suggests otherwise:

1. `overwhelmed` when high tension/load dominates
2. `stuck` when the user is locked on one problem with little progress
3. `distracted` when attention repeatedly shifts
4. `drained` when low energy is the dominant signal

## Energy

- `low`: tired, drained, sleepy, mentally flat
- `normal`: no strong energy signal
- `high`: restless, overactivated, cannot settle

## Tension

- `low`: calm / no meaningful tension signal
- `medium`: frustration, annoyance, moderate pressure
- `high`: panic, acute overload, intense deadline/exam pressure

## Attention

- `okay`: no dominant attention problem
- `scattered`: attention repeatedly shifts or wanders
- `stuck`: attention is locked on one task/problem/loop

## Ambiguity

If an example cannot be labeled consistently by this guide, flag it for review instead of forcing a label.
